# Verified method sources
1. User-supplied book: Meucci, Risk and Asset Allocation, Springer2005. Printed103-105,123-125,233-234. Market invariance, iid horizon projection and conditional variance. Primary textbook. Book excerpt/page pixels inspected in preceding audit; PDF remains private.
2. Gneiting & Raftery(2007), Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction and Estimation, JASA. https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf . Fetched full article. Sections4.2-4.3 support exact CRPS as a proper distribution score; negative-loss orientation used here. Covers discrete support, unlike comparable continuous log-density scoring. Primary journal paper.
3. Patton(2011), Volatility Forecast Comparison Using Imperfect Volatility Proxies. https://public.econ.duke.edu/~ap172/Patton_vol_proxies_JoE_2011.pdf . Fetched full paper. QLIKE ranking with conditionally unbiased noisy variance proxy and its qualifications. Weekly squared residuals are noisy and common means not known truths. Primary journal paper.
4. Bollerslev(1986), Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity. https://public.econ.duke.edu/%7Eboller/Published_Papers/joe_86.pdf . Fetched full paper. Positive variance recursion and stationarity conditions; GARCH(1,1), not asymmetry or guaranteed finite price exponential moments. Primary journal paper.
5. Gneiting, Balabdaoui & Raftery(2007), Probabilistic Forecasts, Calibration and Sharpness. https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jrssb.pdf . Fetched full paper. Calibration plus sharpness, PIT diagnostics limitations; a uniform pooled PIT is not complete conditional calibration. Primary journal paper.
6. arch forecasting documentation. https://arch.readthedocs.io/en/stable/univariate/forecasting.html . Fetched full page. One-step conditional variance timing, analytical/simulation/bootstrap distinction. Official software documentation for independent formula grounding only; arch is not added as a dependency and no code copied.
7. Pritsker/Federal Reserve(2001), The Hidden Dangers of Historical Simulation. https://www.federalreserve.gov/Pubs/feds/2001/200127/200127pap.pdf . Fetched full paper. Historical simulation/FHS and changing dependence/tail sample cautions. Central-bank research.

# Sources and use
- Michaud & Michaud, Estimation Error and Portfolio Optimization (2007 working version), author PDF, https://www.newfrontieradvisors.com/media/rxbld4hq/estimation-error-and-portfolio-optimization-12-05.pdf . Fetched/read abstract and method outline. Motivation for refitting parameters and averaging allocations, not a universal superiority claim; implementation is independent, no article text reproduced.
- Shiraishi/Taniguchi portfolio estimation resampling paper, Japanese Journal of Statistics40(2),2010, publisher PDF https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjss/40/2/40_2_189/_pdf/-char/en . Fetched. Dependence-sensitive resampling and estimation risk; our stationary resampling is not a replication of their VAR/VGARCH estimators.
- Wasserstein mean-variance paper, NSF-hosted primary manuscript https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10344988 . Fetched. Distributional uncertainty changes the optimization; no theorem transferred to a different metric without derivation.
- Jacquier, Simulation-based Estimation in Portfolio Selection, academic-hosted chapter https://people.bu.edu/jacquier/papers/JacPol.bergerchap.2010.pdf . Fetched. Distinguish parameter/model uncertainty from conditional Monte Carlo uncertainty.
- Why estimation alone causes Markowitz portfolio selection to fail, Reading repository primary manuscript https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/107072/1/HS%20article_Ver_3.pdf . Fetched. Supports matched simple baselines and caveat that averaging optimized portfolios is not guaranteed best. No assumed superiority.
- Politis & Romano, The Stationary Bootstrap (1994), DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476870 . DOI fetch returned NO_CONTENT; publisher abstract previously inspected at https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476870 . Random geometric-length blocks and stationarity assumptions; paper not fully fetched. Geometric restart implementation independently tested, not a confidence-validity claim under crisis regime shifts.
- FHS authors' history https://www.filteredhistoricalsimulation.com/history-of-fhs.php . Fetched. Joint parallel residual resampling, conditional volatility; our EWMA variant is explicitly not fitted GARCH or original replication.
- Federal Reserve FEDS2001-27, The Hidden Dangers of Historical Simulation, primary working paper https://www.federalreserve.gov/Pubs/feds/2001/200127/200127pap.pdf . Fetched. FHS limitations: changing correlations and short extreme-tail samples. No claim our finite histories solve these.
- Esfahani & Kuhn2018 https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10107-017-1172-1 . Fetched. Sufficient transport/Lipschitz CVaR bound, not permission to call chosen radius confidence-calibrated. Derive chosen dual norm explicitly and test finite transport LP limits.

All sources are method grounding, not code copied. Existing project officialETF/FX/URA provenance retained. Findings from small conditional pilot runs are not established economic performance or causal tests.

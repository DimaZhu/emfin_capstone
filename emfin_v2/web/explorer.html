Research and science: utility comparison

Definitions, identity and source notes

G is terminal marked wealth, L the terminal payment reserve, X=G-L, and S0=W0-L is the initial surplus used to match local dollar aversion. Native CE uses the provisioning convention; common ledger CE uses the cash-first policy. HARA(G,L,gamma) is CRRA(X,gamma), up to positive scaling. Putting L on X again would count payments twice. For a log case gamma=1, use log. Gross/native and surplus/native CEs are different arguments, not a cross-family ranking.

No economic winner from one retrospective fitted-model study. All raw portfolios and final failures remain recorded. The hard risk constraints and validation grid can overwhelm preference differences. Domain feasibility on finite training paths is not all-support admissibility.
